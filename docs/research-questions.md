# Research Questions

**RQ1:** Does teacher disagreement predict human ambiguity and help distinguish aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty?

**RQ2:** Can a small 1.5B student learn typed span-level uncertainty and improve over its own confidence?

**RQ3:** Can uncertainty-aware routing improve privacy/utility tradeoffs while controlling unnecessary masking and leakage?

**RQ4:** Does the approach generalize from Banglish to Hinglish?
